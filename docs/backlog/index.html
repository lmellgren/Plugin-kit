LOMAS plugin backlog

One row per plugin from lomas/BACKLOG.md (source: the Ableton project 261105 Pose måker.als). For each: a quick mockup in the Seam design system, a complexity assessment, and a proposed approach using the LOMAS recipe (P0–P8, Plugin-kit, measure the installed reference as a black box). Mockups are first sketches built from Seam tokens and components (ChainSlot, Knob, segmented control, LevelMeter, GR meter). They are not frozen faces; each face is frozen in Seam’s design/ before its P7. Names are Seam’s own; the references are internal only. 11 Nov 2026.
Cheapest win: IronA transformer-only module that reuses Pre 73’s transformer and bias model. Could be built right after Pre 73 at about half the effort of Passive EQ.
Most used: Vari-mu14 uses, and its face already exists. It also builds the dynamics framework (detector, GR meter, step-response measurement) that FET and Multiband need.
Reconsider: MultibandA clean digital tool with no analogue character to capture. It has a heavy GUI and gets 1 use. It is better hosted as 3rd party.
Proposed orderPre 73 → Vari-mu → Iron → FET → Saturator → Bass harmonics. Multiband and Harmonics are parked. Tape (T805-style, no transport) is a candidate to unpark. Transient is an optional small filler.
EFX: small pluginsSpring, Ring mod, and the RC-20 modules (Wobble, Distort, Space, Loss) as separate plugins. Ring mod, Wobble and Loss are cheap. Spring is the strongest candidate. Before the first one, Seam needs a decision on where EFX live (insert or send).
Complexity: ●○○○○ trivial … ●●●●● very hard. Effort is relative to LOMAS Passive EQ (= 1×, M0–M7b) and assumes Plugin-kit as it stands after Pre 73.
